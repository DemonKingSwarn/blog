Hey guys, 

So a day back I discovered this amazing app called [Slowly](https://slowly.app/), which is a penpals app. Unlike modern messaging apps which delivers your messages instantly, it simulates the letter style of messaging. The "letters" take hours or days depending on the distance to reach the person you are sending it to. I've found this app actually kind of therapeutic and nice to make some global friends. I've met a person who has so many similar interests as me and also learning German just like me, which makes it so much more easier now. We can just help each other with our German practices in the letters.

The app is available on both [playstore](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.slowlyapp) and [app store](https://apps.apple.com/app/slowly/id1199811908), and it also has a [web version](https://web.slowly.app/), so you can try it on any platform you like.

Anyway, that's all I wanted to share with you all, just that this is my new top 5 apps on mobile now.
